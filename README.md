# Company Knowledge Curation Skill

A standalone, model-agnostic skill for turning raw company materials and approved business updates into a structured, reviewable Company Pack.

这是一个独立的“公司知识整理 / 浓缩 / 更新” Skill。它可以读取 PDF、PPT、Word、Excel、图片、证书、产品目录、网页导出资料以及已确认的公司信息更新候选，经过事实提取、范围判断、去重、冲突检查和人工审核后，生成标准化 Company Pack。

> 核心原则：**先提取与判断，再审核；用户确认后，才进入正式公司知识。**

The repository is intentionally company-agnostic and can be reused by Business AI, sales AI, procurement AI, internal knowledge agents, or other agent systems.
