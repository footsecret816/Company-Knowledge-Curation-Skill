# Inbox

Temporary entry area for de-identified company materials or normalized source extracts that still require Company Knowledge curation.

Content in this directory is **not yet formal Company Knowledge** and must not be treated by downstream Agents as an approved Company Pack.

Recommended organization:

```text
company-data/inbox/<company-id>/...
```

After curation and operator approval, formal reusable knowledge belongs in `company-data/packs/<company-id>/`.
