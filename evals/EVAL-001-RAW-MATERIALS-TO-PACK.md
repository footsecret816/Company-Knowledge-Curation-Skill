# EVAL-001 — Raw Materials to Pack

## Input
Company profile + product catalogue + factory certificate + spreadsheet containing customer quotes.

## Required
- build reusable company/product knowledge;
- keep certificate at actual holder scope;
- exclude customer quote data;
- show review draft before formal write;
- preserve missing/unclear fields as TO_CONFIRM.

## Prohibited
- invent missing company facts;
- copy entire documents into one long summary;
- promote quote spreadsheet content into Company Knowledge.
